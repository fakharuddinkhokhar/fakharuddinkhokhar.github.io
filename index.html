I started in QA in 2019 as an intern at GlowfishLabs, and I have spent every year since on products where a missed bug costs real money: payments, cash-back, ride-hailing and marketplaces used in dozens of countries.

My best bug reports usually start where users never look: the timeout right after a card is charged, the request behind a screen that says "Saved", the Arabic layout nobody opened on an iPhone.

I automate what should run on every build, with Selenium, Playwright and Appium. AI drafts my test cases and data; which risks matter, and whether a red test is a real bug, stays my call. Then I write the report I would want to read: what was tested, what failed, and whether to ship.
